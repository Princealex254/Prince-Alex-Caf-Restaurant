About Us

Prince Alex Café & Restaurant is a culinary gem rooted in the heart of Uthiru, Kenya. We pride ourselves on serving authentic Kenyan cuisine and providing a warm, hospitable dining experience.

Our commitment is to offer **affordable, fresh, and modern dining** where every dish is prepared with passion and local ingredients. Come and enjoy a true taste of Kenya, crafted with community in mind.
